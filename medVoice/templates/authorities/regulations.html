Healthcare Grievance Regulations

This section outlines the regulations governing healthcare grievances and the authority's role in monitoring hospital compliance.

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor hospital response times to patient complaints
  • Escalate complaints not addressed within stipulated timeframes
  • Issue warnings for non-compliance
  • Freeze hospital accounts for severe violations
  • Ensure patient data privacy and confidentiality

Response Time Standards

  • Initial Response: Hospitals must acknowledge complaints within 24 hours
  • Full Response: Detailed response required within 48 hours
  • Resolution: Complaints should be resolved within 7 days

Escalation Criteria

  • Hospital fails to view complaint within 24 hours
  • Hospital fails to respond within 48 hours
  • Patient reports unsatisfactory response
  • Complaint severity is marked as "High"
