quiz Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a complaint? expand_more
You can submit a complaint by clicking the "Post Complaint" button in the sidebar. You will be guided through a simple form where you can provide details about the incident, the healthcare provider involved, and attach any relevant evidence.
Can I post anonymously? expand_more
Yes, protecting your privacy is our priority. Your complaint is publicly displayed as "Anonymous User" by default, though MedVoice administrators may see your details for verification purposes only.
How is my complaint verified? expand_more
Every complaint undergoes a preliminary review by our compliance team. We check for factual consistency and may cross-reference with public hospital records. We may contact you for additional details if needed.
How long does resolution take? expand_more
Resolution times vary. Simple cases are often resolved within 5-7 business days, while complex investigations involving multiple parties may take up to 30 days. You can track progress on your "My Issues" page.
